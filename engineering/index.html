What the labels mean

The atlas is intentionally broader than a case-study page and more honest than a feature checklist. A source module, a passing historical test receipt, a packaged candidate, and a deployed customer system are not the same achievement.

Documented build

Implementation and supporting tests or operating guidance are present in the private engineering record. It is not a claim of customer deployment.

Prototype or research build

The core idea was implemented or exercised, but adoption, portability, or production qualification remains bounded.

Development alpha

A dedicated build exists and executes in a development environment, with release gates still open.

Verified package candidate

A clean package or installer path has its own integrity and installation evidence. Final field acceptance remains separate.

Public descriptions omit private paths, prompts, customer material, credentials, and internal identities. Detailed evidence can be reviewed privately when a real collaboration calls for it.
